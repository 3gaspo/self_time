\maketitle
Self TIME compares frozen Chronos-2 and TS-ICL forecasts with matched forecasts
that receive declared causal transformations of the visible target history as
past covariates. The implemented transformations use normalized magnitude,
normalized sign, the visible raw target, constants, or the declared combined
channel. Missing positions remain missing and no future target is used.
